// HOW TO PLAY

THE ONE RULE: every time you arrive at a node, every edge touching that node flips — walkable (solid green) becomes blocked (dashed grey), and blocked becomes walkable.

tap the lit node to see it flip

You can only step along a solid edge. Landing on a node immediately rewires it, so the path you just walked in on is usually gone behind you.
